# Award-Winning Mac App References

Mac apps that won or were shortlisted for design and app awards from 2023 through 2026, collected as reference implementations for Upshot's macOS design. MacStories Selects is the primary source. Apple's awards are secondary.

Mac availability comes from each app's App Store page or Mac App Store search on 2026-11-14. "Mac App Store" means the app has a Mac listing. "Apple silicon only" means the Mac listing requires an Apple M1 chip or later. "Designed for iPad" means the App Store page says "Designed for iPad. Not verified for macOS", so the app is an iPad app running on Mac and is a weak macOS reference. "Developer website" means the MacStories article links the developer's website instead of an App Store listing.

## MacStories Selects

MacStories Selects 2026 has not been published. The 2025 article appeared in December 2025.

### Best Mac App

- 2025 winner: [Bloom](https://bloomapp.club/), a Finder replacement. Developer website. MacStories cites 12 pane layouts saved as Workspaces, a Portal mini window that can be pinned above other windows, a menu bar app, built-in image editing tools, and keyboard shortcuts for almost every action.
- 2025 runner-up: [Supercharge](https://sindresorhus.com/supercharge) by Sindre Sorhus. Developer website. MacStories cites one menu bar icon collecting many utilities, a keyboard shortcut for each utility, Shortcuts actions, and a built-in tool that finds keyboard shortcut conflicts.
- 2024 winner: [Moom 4](https://manytricks.com/moom/), a window manager. Developer website. MacStories cites a customizable command palette that appears when hovering over a window's green button, a grid on which the user draws a rectangle to move and resize a window, and Hover, which moves windows with a modifier key and the pointer.
- 2024 runner-up: [Bezel](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bezel-phone-mirror/id6476268685), which mirrors an iPhone to the Mac. Mac App Store. MacStories cites screenshots placed in a device frame with configurable padding, color, pattern, or transparency, keyboard shortcuts for almost every action, and resizing the window to the device's pixel size.
- 2023 winner: [Mimestream](https://mimestream.com/), a Gmail client. Developer website. MacStories cites a thoroughly native interface from a team led by a former Apple Mail engineer, profiles that group accounts, and extensive settings.
- 2023 runner-up: [Bartender 5](https://www.macbartender.com/), a menu bar manager. Developer website. MacStories cites menu bar style settings for color, border, drop shadow, and shape, icon groups, and triggers that show or hide icons.

### Best Design

- 2025 winner: [Sequel](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sequel-media-tracker/id1630746993), a media tracker. Apple silicon only. MacStories cites its Liquid Glass adoption, universal search in the Mac sidebar, and a detail view whose header image turns into a blurred background as the view scrolls.
- 2025 runner-up: [(Not Boring) Camera](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/not-boring-camera/id6737783441). Designed for iPad.
- 2024 winner: [Shareshot](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/frame-screenshots-shareshot/id6474271573), which frames screenshots. Mac App Store, requires macOS 26.0 or later. MacStories cites detection of a screenshot on the clipboard, a segmented control for background options, one editing view with controls for background, drop shadow, and padding, and output that looks good every time.
- 2024 runner-up: [Reeder](https://apps.apple.com/app/reeder/id6475002485), a feed reader. Mac App Store. MacStories cites a clear layout, adjustable text, and simple animations.
- 2023 winner: [Mercury Weather](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mercury-weather/id1621800675). Mac App Store. MacStories cites gradients, typography, white space, a forecast graph that is never crowded, and that graph in the macOS menu bar.
- 2023 runner-up: [Matter](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/matter-a-better-reading-app/id1501592184), a reading app. Designed for iPad.
